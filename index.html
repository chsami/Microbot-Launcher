Microbot account

Your account for Microbot itself: this launcher and the Microbot dashboard.

Is it my RuneScape or Jagex account?
No. It's a separate account with its own email and password, so don't reuse your Jagex password. Signing in doesn't share your game accounts with Microbot.
What do I get?
One sign-in for the launcher and the dashboard at dashboard.microbot.cloud. Coming soon: back up your profiles and use them on another PC.
Do I need one?
No. The launcher and the client work without an account. Signing in is optional.
What do you store?
Your email address and your password in hashed form. Nothing else is needed.

Your proxy is used for Jagex login

  • Logging in with a Jagex account opens a separate private browser window instead of your normal browser.
  • That window and the launcher's Jagex requests go through this proxy, so Jagex sees the proxy's IP instead of yours. You don't need Proxifier or a proxy browser extension.
  • The window starts without your cookies or history and is deleted after you log in.
  • It needs Chrome, Edge, Brave or another Chromium-based browser installed.
  • Pick "No proxy" to log in with your normal browser.

Setup guide

    Welcome to Microbot

    You can be playing in a few minutes. First, find out which account you have. You don't need all three.

    Most players

    Jagex account

    You log in on account.jagex.com or with the Jagex Launcher, usually with an authenticator code. New accounts are Jagex accounts.

    Older accounts

    Normal account

    An older account you still log in to by typing your username or email and password on the game's login screen.

    Optional

    Microbot account

    Your account for this launcher and the Microbot dashboard. It is not a game account and you don't need it to play.

    Not sure? If the game's login screen tells you to use the Jagex Launcher, you have a Jagex account.

    Add your Jagex account

    1. Using a proxy? Pick it first. The Jagex login then goes through the same proxy as the game. Skip this if you don't use one.
    2. Click Login Jagex Account. If you already added characters, click Add accounts instead.
    3. Log in on the Jagex page. It opens in your browser, or in a separate private window if you use a proxy. Enter your email, password and authenticator code there. Microbot never sees your password.
    4. Come back to the launcher. When the login finishes, every character on that Jagex account shows up in the Jagex Account list.
    5. Pick a character and click Play With Jagex Account.
    • Have more Jagex accounts? Log in once per account with Add accounts.
    • Changed a display name or added a character? Click the refresh button next to the list.
    • Login stuck? Click Cancel login and try again.

    Play with a normal account

    You don't add normal accounts to the launcher. You log in inside the game, like you always did.

    1. Click Play Normal Account. The client opens on the game's login screen.
    2. Log in on that screen with your username or email and password.
    • Play Normal Account also opens the client without logging in, for example to set up plugins first.
    • Once an account is moved to a Jagex account, it can't go back. Use the Jagex account steps for it from then on.

    Do I need a Microbot account?

    No. The launcher and the client work without one.

    What is it for?
    One sign-in for this launcher and the dashboard at dashboard.microbot.cloud. Coming soon: back up your profiles and use them on another PC.
    Is it my Jagex account?
    No. It has its own email and password. Don't reuse your Jagex password.
    Does Microbot see my game accounts?
    No. Signing in doesn't share your game accounts with Microbot.

    Check these before you play

    1. Version. Keep the newest one. Older versions stop working after game updates.
    2. Profile. Holds your plugin settings. Each Jagex character remembers its own profile, so you can keep a separate setup per account.
    3. Client RAM. 1 or 2 GB is enough for one client. Raise it if the client lags or closes with an out of memory error.
    4. Proxy. Optional, SOCKS5 only. Characters you added through a proxy switch back to it when you select them.

    Stay safe and get help

    • Nobody from Microbot will ask for your password or authenticator codes. Not in Discord, not in a DM.
    • Only download Microbot from themicrobot.com. Copies from other places can steal your accounts.
    • Botting breaks the game's rules. Any account you bot on can be banned, so don't bot on an account you can't afford to lose.
    • Something not working? Open the menu at the top left, then Open, then Launcher Logs or Client Logs. Share them on Discord when you ask for help.
    • Want this guide again? It's in the same menu under Setup guide.
